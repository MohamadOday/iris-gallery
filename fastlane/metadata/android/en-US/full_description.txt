Iris Gallery is a lightweight, fluid, and privacy-focused Android gallery app built with modern Material You 3 and Jetpack Compose.

Features:
• 100% Offline & Private: Zero INTERNET permissions requested in the manifest. Your photos and videos never leave your device.
• Fluid Material Design 3: Full dynamic Material You theming that adapts to your wallpaper, complete with seamless dark and light modes.
• High-Resolution Canvas Viewer: Native hardware bitmap rendering with multi-level zoom (up to 7×) preserving crystal-clear detail on screenshots, text, and photos.
• Built-in Photo Editor: Crop, rotate (90°/180°/270°), flip horizontally/vertically, apply filters, sketch with custom brush tools, and add text.
• Rich Camera EXIF & GPS Inspector: View detailed camera metadata including model, aperture, shutter speed, focal length, ISO, flash, and open capture coordinates directly in your preferred map app.
• Integrated Video Player: Powered by Media3 ExoPlayer with sensor-aware hardware rotation and intuitive playback controls.
• Dynamic Pinch-to-Resize Grid: Smoothly adjust photo column density between 2 and 6 columns with responsive item placement animations.
• Media Formats & Special Types: Dedicated smart filters for RAW photos, animated GIFs, motion photos, panoramas, and screenshots.
• Duplicate & Similar Media Finder: Scan for identical or redundant shots and clean up storage with one tap.
• Private Locked Vault: Protect sensitive photos and videos behind biometric authentication or device credentials.
• Safe Trash Bin: Move deleted items to the trash with easy restoration or one-tap permanent purge.
• Home Screen Photo Widget: Pin favorite memories and photos directly to your launcher with customizable aspect ratios and rotation intervals.
